Fabrics we work with

Natural cloth that breathes, wears well and looks better with age.

  • Khadi

    Hand-spun, hand-woven cotton. Breathable and lightly textured, and softer with every wash.

  • Handloom cotton

    Soft and light. It holds block prints well and suits daily wear.

  • Linen

    Cool and crisp for hot, humid days. It creases, and that is part of its look.

  • Raw silk

    A textured silk with a quiet sheen. Our choice for festive and wedding pieces.

Why Abubakar Clothes

Abubakar Clothes began with a simple belief: good clothes should carry a story. Each piece blends Indian craftsmanship with a modern fit, made for people who value comfort, dignity and style.

We use natural fabrics, traditional prints and clean cuts, so a piece looks right at a wedding and still feels right on an ordinary Friday.
